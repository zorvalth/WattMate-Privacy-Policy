WattMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: December 2024

Summary: WattMate collects only the data necessary to track your EV charging sessions. Your data is stored securely in the cloud and is never sold to third parties.

1. Introduction

WattMate ("we", "our", or "the app") is an electric vehicle charging tracker application. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our app.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Email address: Used for account creation and authentication
  • Display name: Optional, used for personalization

2.2 Vehicle Information

  • Vehicle make, model, and year
  • Battery capacity
  • Odometer readings
  • Efficiency data

2.3 Charging Session Data

  • Charging location names (entered by you)
  • Energy consumed (kWh)
  • Charging costs
  • Date and time of sessions
  • State of charge (start/end percentages)

2.4 Preferences

  • Display units (km/miles)
  • Currency preference
  • Language preference

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and maintain the WattMate service
  • Calculate charging statistics and efficiency metrics
  • Sync your data across your devices
  • Improve the app experience

4. Data Storage and Security

Your data is stored securely using Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Authentication: Manages your account securely
  • Cloud Firestore: Stores your vehicle and charging data

All data is encrypted in transit and at rest. We follow industry-standard security practices to protect your information.

5. Third-Party Services

WattMate uses the following third-party services:

  • Google Firebase: Authentication, database, and app configuration
  • Google Sign-In: Optional authentication method

These services have their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review them:

  • Firebase Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

6. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties.

We may share your information only:

  • With your consent
  • To comply with legal obligations
  • To protect our rights or the safety of users

7. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: View all your data within the app
  • Export: Export your data in JSON format
  • Delete: Delete your account and all associated data
  • Correct: Update your information at any time

How to Delete Your Account

You can delete your account and all associated data from within the app:

  1. Go to Settings
  2. Select "Account"
  3. Tap "Delete Account"
  4. Confirm deletion

This action is permanent and cannot be undone. All your vehicles, charging sessions, and preferences will be deleted.

8. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all your data is permanently deleted from our servers within 30 days.

9. Children's Privacy

WattMate is not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy in the app or via email.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, please contact us at:

Email: support@wattmate.app
